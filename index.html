    Cheat sheet
    
    
    The constants worth memorising, grouped the way you reach for them.
    
  
    
  
    
    
    Circuits
    • V = I · R
    • P = V · I
    • τ = R · C
    • fc = 1 / (2πRC)
    • XC = 1 / (2πfC)
    
    
    Embedded
    • f = fclk/((PSC+1)(ARR+1))
    • Duty = CCR/(ARR+1)
    • LSB = Vref/(2ⁿ−1)
    • UART 115200 baud ≈ 11.5 kB/s
    • Set bit n: REG |= (1U << n)
    • Clear bit n: REG &= ~(1U << n)
    
    
    Signals
    • +3 dB ≈ 2 × power
    • +6 dB ≈ 2 × voltage
    • 0 dBm = 1 mW
    • 30 dBm = 1 W
    
    
    Everyday
    • 1 in = 25.4 mm
    • 1 atm = 101 325 Pa
    • 1 kWh = 3.6 MJ
    • °F = °C × 9/5 + 32
    • 1 Ah = 3600 C
